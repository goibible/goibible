# GOI English NT Bucket 4 Inspection

Scope: remaining NT source-name rows after accepting form variants, compound names, common-noun contexts, and acceptable compression.

## Findings

### JHN 13:1

- Source has explicit `Jesus` (`G2424`).
- GOI English: `Now before the feast of the Passover, knowing that his hour had come...`
- KJV and WEBUS both keep `Jesus`.
- Recommendation: edit GOI English to include `Jesus`.

### ACT 4:6

- Source has `Jerusalem` (`G2419`): `eis Ierousalem`.
- GOI English omits location: `Annas the high priest... high-priestly family.`
- KJV includes `at Jerusalem`; WEBUS omits it.
- Recommendation: likely edit GOI English to include `at Jerusalem`, because the TR source includes it.

### ACT 9:29

- Source has `Lord Jesus` (`G2962`, `G2424`) in `in the name of the Lord Jesus`.
- GOI English omits the phrase: `He spoke boldly and disputed with the Grecians...`
- KJV and WEBUS include `in the name of the Lord Jesus`.
- Recommendation: edit GOI English. This is a real omission.

### ACT 13:33

- Source has explicit `God` (`G2316`) and `Jesus` (`G2424`).
- GOI English starts after that clause: `As it has also been written...`
- KJV and WEBUS include God fulfilling/raising Jesus before the Psalm quote.
- Recommendation: edit GOI English. This is a real omission.

## Summary

- Clear edit candidates: `JHN 13:1`, `ACT 9:29`, `ACT 13:33`.
- Likely edit candidate: `ACT 4:6`.
- Resolved textual-policy case: `ROM 12:11` follows the stronger critical manuscript reading `κυρίῳ` / `Lord` by project decision; GOI English now reads `serving the Lord`.
