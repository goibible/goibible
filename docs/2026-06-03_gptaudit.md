## Clean / Low-Concern Patterns

- The sampled `wife/woman` and `husband/man` renderings were mostly sound; the largest remaining risk is not gender assignment but localized clause corruption around those verses.
- `God/god/gods`, `Lord/master`, and `Spirit/spirit` looked mostly stable in the spot checks that were directly reviewed here.
- Several awkward lines are still intelligible; the report above prioritizes verses where awkwardness appears to hide a real semantic error.

## Open Questions

- `1TI 3:11` still has the interpretation question `wives` vs `women`, but `not devils` is wrong under either reading.
- `ROM 3:25` may be intentionally formal, but its present wording is opaque enough that it should be reviewed as an integrity issue, not just a style issue.
- A broader scan for the same false-friend patterns may uncover more defects than the individual verses listed here.

## Recommended Next Pass

- Search systematically for false-friend renderings around these Greek senses:
  - `ἀφίημι` rendered as `forgive` where the context is `leave/allow`
  - `θεραπεία` rendered as `healing` where the context is `household/service`
  - `τιμή` rendered as `honor` where the context is `price/value`
  - `συλλαμβάνω` rendered as `seize` where the context is `conceive`
  - `παρακαλέω` rendered as `encourage` where the context is `beg/implore`
  - `λόγον` rendered as `word` inside accounting idioms
- Search for duplicated medical nouns like `disease ... disease` and other same-token repetitions that likely indicate dropped contrast pairs.
- After those targeted fixes, re-read the immediately surrounding paragraphs of each flagged verse to catch local discourse awkwardness created by ultra-literal pronouning.

## Resolution (2026-06-03, Claude)

**Verdict: AGREE on all 23 findings.** Each was verified against TR1550 + the
public-domain KJV and WEBUS; every one is a genuine defect. All 23 fixed in
`GOI_Bible_English/`.

Categories confirmed:
- **False-friend mistranslations** (clear sense errors vs both references):
  ἀφίημι→"forgive" (MRK 12:19, MRK 5:19, LUK 11:42), θεραπεία→"healing"
  (MAT 24:45, LUK 12:42), συλλαμβάνω→"seized" (LUK 1:24), τιμή→"honor"
  (ACT 5:2), παρακαλέω→"encouraged" (MAT 18:32), διάβολος→"devils" (1TI 3:11),
  γίνομαι→"happened" (2PE 2:1 → "arose"), λόγος→"word" in idioms (MAT 25:19,
  MAT 5:32), παρέρχομαι→"pass away" (LUK 12:37).
- **Clause corruption:** MAT 9:35 "disease ... disease" → "disease ... sickness"
  (this one the multiplicity count-audit could not catch — the count matched).
  MRK 12:19 also restored the dropped "no children" negation.
- **Restored a 2nd dropped noun:** 1TI 3:11 also recovered νηφάλιος ("temperate").
- **Awkward/over-literal:** MAT 22:27, ROM 7:2, LUK 1:18, MAT 19:3, ROM 3:25
  ("set forth as a propitiation"), and ἐκεῖνος→"that one's" → "his"
  (2CO 8:9, TIT 3:7, JHN 20:15).

Five fixes changed a noun's rendering away from its strongs default; these were
recorded as `verse_rendering_overrides` (θεραπεία→household ×2, λόγος→cause,
ἡμέρα→years, ἱλαστήριον→propitiation) so noun coverage stays at 0 missing / 99.7%.

**Open patterns worth a future targeted scan** (per the audit's recommendation):
the same false-friends (ἀφίημι, θεραπεία, τιμή, παρακαλέω, λόγος-in-idiom) and
the ἐκεῖνος "that one's" pronouning may recur elsewhere; not yet swept corpus-wide.
