King Ahaz said to Urijah the priest, Offer on the great altar the burnt offering of the morning and the grain offering of the evening, and the burnt offering of the king and his grain offering, and the burnt offering of all the people of the land and their grain offering and their drink offerings; all the blood of the burnt offering and all the blood of the sacrifice you shall sprinkle on it; but the bronze altar shall be for me to inquire.
